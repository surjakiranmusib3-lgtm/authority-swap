# Authority Swap | Revised sprint plan

**14 November 2026 · Planning only · All session times are IST**

## Must-have

**Question.** Does claimed expertise change how often a model abandons a correct answer when the factual rebuttal stays fixed? Frame this as a focused replication or extension. Yang et al. already vary authority framing with fixed rebuttals. Our proposed difference is crossing expertise with tone in one follow-up. Its novelty is unverified. [Closest study](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.08840)

**Design.** Freeze 80–100 verified factual questions and two model configurations. Choose one focal model before seeing pushback outcomes. Clone each correct baseline into four independent branches crossing expert or ordinary speaker with assertive or tentative tone. Add a neutral reconsideration control. Hold the false claim, any rationale, and answer instructions fixed across rebuttal branches. Save keys, prompts, settings, outputs, and failures.

**Primary contrast.** On the focal model, estimate expert minus ordinary false-switch rates, averaging paired differences across tones within each question. Report a question-level 95% confidence interval, baseline accuracy, and eligible counts. At paired variance 0.20, 60–75 eligible questions give approximately 80% power for a 15–16 percentage-point effect. Five-point effects are unlikely to be detected reliably. Treat other contrasts as secondary.
